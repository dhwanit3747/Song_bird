3. STFT spectral noise gate

X(k,m) = STFT{ybp[n]}   ·   N(k) = α P20(|X(k,m)|)   ·   G(k,m) = max(1 − N(k) / max(|X(k,m)|, ε), Gmin)
G is median-smoothed across 5 adjacent time frames   ·   Y(k,m) = G(k,m)X(k,m)   ·   yclean[n] = ISTFT{Y(k,m)}

Noise magnitudes are in normalized STFT amplitude units. Gain statistics describe the time-frequency mask after smoothing; they are not a direct measure of noise removed.

4. Playback normalization and output

yplay[n] = 0.98 · yclean[n] / max(|yclean[n]|)   ·   resampling = rational polyphase resampling when required

This is bird-focused enhancement, not perfect source separation. Calls and traffic can overlap in frequency, so some background may remain and quiet calls may be attenuated.
